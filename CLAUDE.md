# DynALisp

Follow the conventions of DynALisp:

@CONVENTIONS.md

`CONVENTIONS.md` is the core. The larger topics (presets and materials, windows, docs, ribbon, versions, Claude, simulation, project files, spell dictionaries) keep their full rules in a file of their own, `Conventions\<Topic>.md`, listed in the table at the top of `CONVENTIONS.md`: read the file of a topic before changing anything it covers, even when its summary in the core seems enough.

When a new convention is agreed (a rule for how programs, windows, settings or data files are made), add it to `CONVENTIONS.md`, or to the file of its topic, in its section and with the reason for it, in the same change. A rule every change must know goes in the core, with a line in the summary of its topic when it has one. These files are the reference for the conventions: keep them up to date when one changes.
